We are thrilled to finally share **Glosso Studio** with the world. 

Our mission is to provide high-quality, professional-grade pronunciation training that respects your privacy and works wherever you are—even without an internet connection.

### What makes Glosso Studio different?

Most language learning apps use "Speech to Text" engines designed for dictation. While great for typing hands-free, they are often too "forgiving" for pronunciation practice. 

Glosso Studio uses **Phonetic Recognition**. We break down your speech into its smallest components (phonemes) and compare them to native models. This allows us to tell you exactly *which* sound you need to work on.

### Key Highlights:
- **Offline First:** No data leaves your device.
- **Scientific Approach:** Powered by Allosaurus.
- **Free and Open Source:** Built for the community.
